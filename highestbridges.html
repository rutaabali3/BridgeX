Top 10 Highest Bridges in the World

1. Huajiang Canyon Bridge (China)

Height: 625 m (2,051 ft)
Type: Suspension bridge
Location: Guizhou, China
Opened: 2025

The Huajiang Canyon Bridge (Huajiang Grand Canyon Bridge) opened on 28 September 2025 on the S57 Liuzhi–Anlong Expressway, surpassing Duge Bridge to become the world's highest bridge deck at 625 meters above the Beipan River. It has a total length of 2,890 meters and a main span of 1,420 meters. See changelog update.

2. Duge Bridge (China)

Height: 565 m (1,854 ft)
Type: Cable-stayed bridge
Location: Guizhou-Yunnan, China
Opened: 2016

The Duge Bridge, also known as the Beipanjiang Bridge, was the world's highest bridge from 2016 until 2025, towering 565 meters above the Beipan River. It is part of the G56 Hangzhou-Ruili Expressway and cut travel time between Xuanwei and Shuicheng from over 4 hours to about 1 hour. It was surpassed by the Huajiang Canyon Bridge in September 2025. Why the record changed.

3. Tianmen Bridge (China)

Height: ~560 m (1,837 ft)
Type: Suspension bridge
Location: Guizhou, China
Opened: 2026

The Tianmen Bridge carries the Anpan Expressway high above the Beipan River gorge in Guizhou province with a main span of 820 meters. Completed in 2026, it ranks third among the world's highest bridges just behind Huajiang and Duge. See changelog update.

4. Sidu River Bridge (China)

Height: 496 m (1,627 ft)
Type: Suspension bridge
Location: Hubei, China
Opened: 2009

The Sidu River Bridge held the title of world's highest bridge from 2009 to 2016. Spanning the deep Sidu River valley, this suspension bridge is part of the G50 Shanghai-Chongqing Expressway and features a main span of 900 meters. Its construction required rockets to launch the initial guide cables across the gorge.

5. Puli Bridge (China)

Height: 485 m (1,591 ft)
Type: Suspension bridge
Location: Yunnan, China
Opened: 2015

The Puli Bridge crosses the Nizhu River gorge in Yunnan province and forms part of the G56 Hangzhou-Ruili Expressway. With a main span of 628 meters, this suspension bridge connects the cities of Qujing and Kunming, dramatically improving transportation in this mountainous region.

6. Jin'an Bridge (China)

Height: 461 m (1,512 ft)
Type: Suspension bridge
Location: Yunnan, China
Opened: 2020

The Jin'an Bridge spans the Jinsha River in Yunnan province and features a main span of 1,386 meters, making it one of the longest high-altitude suspension bridges in the world as well. This bridge carries the G4216 Chengdu–Lijiang (Huaping–Lijiang) Expressway and significantly improved connectivity in northwest Yunnan. Expressway name updated.

7. Yachi River Bridge (China)

Height: 434 m (1,424 ft) above canyon floor (306 m above reservoir)
Type: Cable-stayed bridge
Location: Guizhou, China
Opened: 2016

The Yachi River Bridge is a cable-stayed bridge with a main span of 800 meters. It forms part of the Qianxi-Guiyang Expressway and crosses the Yachi River in Guizhou province. Its deck rises 434 meters above the original river gorge floor (306 meters above the Dongfeng Dam reservoir surface). Height baseline clarified.

8. Zangkejiang Bridge (China)

Height: ~410 m (1,345 ft)
Type: Suspension bridge
Location: Guizhou, China
Opened: 2025

The Zangkejiang Bridge opened in February 2025 as part of the Nayong–Xingyi Expressway, crossing a deep Beipan River rift with a 1,080-meter main span. See changelog update.

9. Qingshui River Bridge (China)

Height: 406 m (1,332 ft)
Type: Suspension bridge
Location: Guizhou, China
Opened: 2015

The Qingshui River Bridge spans the Qingshui River gorge with a main span of 1,130 meters. This suspension bridge is part of the Guiyang-Qianxi Expressway and features two H-shaped towers that rise dramatically from the valley below.

10. Baluarte Bridge (Mexico)

Height: 403 m (1,322 ft)
Type: Cable-stayed bridge
Location: Durango-Sinaloa, Mexico
Opened: 2012

The Baluarte Bridge is the highest bridge in the Americas and part of the Durango-Mazatlán highway. This cable-stayed bridge spans the Baluarte River and features a total length of 1,124 meters with a central span of 520 meters and a deck height of 403 meters (earlier listings cited 390 m). Following the 2018 earthquake removal of Hegigio Gorge Pipeline Bridge, Baluarte holds #10 in the updated ranking. See changelog update.
